# Sentinel AI — Phase 3 → Sprint 5

### ROLE

Act as the lead architect and engineer for Sentinel AI, an enterprise KYC/AML and due-diligence platform designed for banks, trade-finance firms, and financial institutions.

# STEP 2 — MATCHING & SCORING ENGINE

## Goal

Determine how strongly a target matches a candidate from each source.

Use a structured and explainable scoring system.

Required signals include:

### Name signals

* Jaro-Winkler
* Levenshtein
* normalized name similarity
* alias similarity
* phonetic similarity

### Attribute signals

* birth year agreement
* date-of-birth agreement
* country/jurisdiction agreement
* nationality where applicable
* identifier agreement
* entity-type agreement
* gender where applicable

### Mismatch signals

Examples:

* gender discrepancy
* clear DOB mismatch
* conflicting entity type
* conflicting organizational attributes
* other strong identity contradictions

### Hard-match signal

Exact identifier matches must receive special treatment.

---

# SCORING RULES

Do NOT use:

```text
simple average
simple maximum
```

for cross-source consensus.

Source-level candidate scoring must be deterministic, explainable, validated, and testable.

Scores must remain within:

```text
0.0 → 1.0
```

The system must reject invalid scores.

Duplicate source records must be prevented where a source is expected to be unique.

---

# LLM / AI USE

LLMs may be used where they genuinely improve:

* interpretation of messy names
* linguistic/alias interpretation
* explainability
* candidate-context interpretation
* normalization assistance
* ambiguous entity-name analysis

However:

**Do not allow an LLM to arbitrarily decide a sanctions hit without structured evidence.**

The final screening decision must remain controlled by the deterministic scoring/rules framework and auditable evidence.

AI should augment the engine, not make the compliance decision opaque.

# STEP 6 — CONSENSUS RISK / CRI

## Goal

After source-level screening is reliable, combine source evidence into one consensus risk measure.

Required formula:

```text
CRI = [1 - Π(1 - (Si × Wi))] × 100
```

Where:

```text
Si = validated source match score
Wi = source weight
```

Do not replace this with:

```text
simple average
simple maximum
```

Validate:

```text
0 <= score <= 1
0 <= weight <= 1
duplicate sources rejected
```

---

# DEFAULT VERDICTS

Use:

```text
CRI >= 85
→ FLAGGED_HIGH_RISK

CRI >= 65
→ POTENTIAL_MATCH_REVIEW

CRI < 65
→ CLEAR
```

These are defaults and must remain configurable.

---

# HARD RISK OVERRIDE

If an exact identifier match is established:

```text
minimum score = 0.85
```

and:

```text
FLAGGED_HIGH_RISK
```

must be enforced.

A normal low CRI must not override a confirmed exact identifier match.
